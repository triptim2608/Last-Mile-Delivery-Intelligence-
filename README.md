# Last-Mile Delivery Intelligence 
Operational SLA Monitoring & Delivery Performance Optimization Dashboard

# Problem Statement
Zomato’s last-mile delivery operations face challenges related to SLA breaches, delivery delays, rider inefficiency, traffic congestion, weather disruptions, and inconsistent delivery performance across geographic regions. The objective of this project was to analyze operational data to identify key delay drivers, monitor delivery efficiency, and improve decision-making through interactive business intelligence dashboards.

# Project Description
- Developed a multi-page enterprise-style Power BI dashboard solution to analyze Zomato’s delivery operations, rider productivity, customer experience, and geographic delivery performance using Power BI, SQL, DAX, and Power Query.
- I Focused on KPI governance, SLA monitoring, operational intelligence, and performance benchmarking by implementing interactive dashboards, drill-through navigation, heatmaps, scatter plots, matrices, and conditional formatting.
- I Learned advanced Power BI concepts including DAX measures, data modeling, relationship management, drill-through reporting, dynamic filtering, KPI storytelling, operational dashboard design, and executive reporting frameworks.
- Applied business intelligence and exploratory data analysis techniques to transform raw operational datasets into actionable insights for delivery optimization and performance monitoring.

# Key Insights
- Traffic congestion and multiple simultaneous deliveries were identified as major contributors to SLA breaches and delivery delays.
- Semi-urban delivery zones showed higher operational risk and slower average delivery performance compared to metropolitan regions.
- Delivery turnaround time increased significantly during adverse weather conditions and peak traffic hours.
- Rider efficiency varied across delivery zones, highlighting opportunities for operational standardization and workload optimization.
# Recommendations
- Optimize rider allocation during peak traffic hours to reduce SLA breach probability.
- Implement zone-based delivery planning for semi-urban regions to improve operational efficiency.
- Introduce dynamic route optimization and workload balancing strategies for high-risk delivery conditions.
- Monitor rider productivity using KPI-driven dashboards to improve delivery consistency and customer experience.
